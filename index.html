Features

Built for the way you actually drive.

Every feature designed around one question: how do we make your next drive shorter, safer, and more pleasant than your last one?

Predictive AI routing

On-device traffic model reroutes you around jams before they happen, learning your driving habits over time.

Live community reports

Police, accidents, hazards, and cameras — broadcast in real-time from every driver around you.

Voice-first co-pilot

"Find cheap gas under $4." "Avoid the highway." WIZI handles it. Always hands-free.

Beautiful real maps

A real MapLibre map with a custom WIZI dark style, traffic heatmap, and report pins — no API key needed.

Smarter alternatives

Compare routes side-by-side with live ETA, traffic level, and CO₂ estimate.

Offline-ready

Download entire regions for turn-by-turn navigation without a signal — perfect for road trips.

Safety alerts

Speed limit warnings, dangerous turns, school zones, and accident-prone areas, surfaced before you reach them.

EV mode

Route through real-time charger availability with smart range-aware stops.

Group driving

Share your live trip, drive in convoy with friends, and chat without leaving the map.
